# WorkSphere Payroll Automation v11

- Attendance and approved leave now feed payroll automatically.
- Admin/HR can configure working days, standard hours, paid leave and overtime policy under Settings.
- Unpaid Leave / LOP and approved unpaid absence are deducted automatically.
- Overtime is optional and policy-controlled.
- Current-month payroll only counts elapsed working days; closed months use the configured monthly working-day divisor.
- Payroll stores an attendance snapshot so historical salary slips remain reproducible.
- Salary slips show attendance deduction and overtime separately.
- Employee cannot modify payroll or attendance timestamps through Firestore rules.
