# Reports fixed

The Reports hub now loads live Firestore data and exposes role-appropriate reports.

- Admin/HR/Manager: Employee, Attendance, Leave reports
- Admin/HR: Payroll report
- Documents/Departments/Requests open their live management screens
- Reports overview shows employee count, attendance records, leave requests, net payroll and status counters
- `/reports/employees` route is now registered
- Management users see company attendance/leave data instead of only their own records
- Employee report filters workforce records to employee profiles
