# WorkSphere Leave Approval Policy

- Employee: can apply, view, edit/cancel pending own leave. Cannot approve or reject leave.
- Manager: can review pending leave requests available to management and approve/reject them.
- HR: can review and approve/reject leave requests.
- Admin: full leave management and can approve/reject leave requests.

Approval/rejection is enforced by Firestore management permissions; the UI buttons are only shown to management roles.
