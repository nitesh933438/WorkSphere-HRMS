# WorkSphere v17 — Workflow Fixes

This release makes the main workplace systems role-aware and data-connected.

- Requests: employees submit/manage their own pending requests; Admin/HR/Manager see workforce requests and approve/reject them.
- Attendance: employees use check-in/check-out and own history; management sees workforce attendance with employee filtering.
- Documents: employees see their own documents; management sees workforce documents with category and employee filtering.
- Departments: employee directory department filters are synchronized with the department master list and employee realtime updates.
- Announcements: all roles can read published announcements; management creates/edits/deletes; drafts stay management-only; published announcements generate company notifications.
- Security: employee request/leave accounts cannot change reviewer/approval fields or self-approve.
- PWA version: 1.0.15.
