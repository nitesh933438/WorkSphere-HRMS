- Reports

- Departments
- Announcements

- Documents

- Payroll / Payroll History / Generate Payroll

- Employees
- Attendance
- Documents

- Requests

## Employee
- Dashboard
- Attendance
- Documents
- Leave / Apply Leave
- Salary Slip
- Requests
- Notifications
- Profile / Settings

Leave is available to **all four roles**. Employees use it to apply and track their own leave; management roles use it to review/manage workforce leave according to their permissions.
